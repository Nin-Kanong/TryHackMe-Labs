## Task 1: Junior Security Analyst Journey

### Security Analyst Journey

A Security Analyst protects an organisation by monitoring systems, investigating suspicious activity, detecting cyberattacks, and responding to security incidents.

#### Main responsibilities

- Monitor security alerts and logs.
- Identify suspicious activities and threats.
- Investigate security incidents.
- Respond to and contain attacks.
- Help protect company systems and data.

**Goal**: Prevent security breaches and keep the organisation safe.

### Your Daily Duties

#### Main daily responsibilities

1. **Monitor Alerts**: Review SIEM alerts and identify suspicious activities.
2. **Investigate Incidents**: Analyse logs and investigate potential threats.
3. **Team Collaboration**: Participate in SOC meetings and workshops.
4. **Work with Other Teams**: Cooperate with IT and security teams.
5. **Continuous Learning**: Learn about new cyberattacks, threats, and defenses.

**Goal**: Detect threats early, investigate incidents, and help protect the organisation.

### SOC and Your Team

A **Security Operations Center (SOC)** is a team responsible for monitoring, detecting, investigating, and responding to cyber threats.

#### Main SOC Team Members

| Role                | Responsibility                                        |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| SOC Level 1 Analyst | Monitor alerts and perform initial investigations.    |
| SOC Level 2 Analyst | Investigate complex incidents and handle escalations. |
| SOC Level 3 Analyst | Handle advanced threats and threat hunting.           |
| SOC Engineer        | Configure and maintain security tools.                |
| SOC Manager         | Manage the SOC team and operations.                   |

### Your Daily Duties

As a **Junior Security Analyst**, we gain practical experience handling real-world cybersecurity incidents.

#### Possible Daily Tasks

1. **Malware Detection**: Detect and prevent data stealer infections.
2. **Phishing Investigation**: Analyse and stop phishing campaigns.
3. **Incident Response**: Assist with major incidents such as ransomware attacks.
4. **Detection Engineering**: Work with teammates to create detection rules and automation.
5. **Business Understanding**: Learn how cybersecurity protects business operations.

> Gain hands-on SOC experience, improve your security analysis skills, and prepare for advanced cybersecurity roles.

## Task 3: A Day in the life of a Security Analyst



<img width="1140" height="801" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/31a9e35a-8a9e-444d-a782-90d0b40cbba5" />


### Being a Security Analyst

A Security Analyst works on the defensive frontline, protecting an organisation from cyber threats during an 8-hour shift.

1. Ticket Management: Review and resolve security alerts and assigned tasks.
2. Threat Detection: Identify suspicious activities and potential attacks.
3. Incident Response: Investigate and stop threats before they damage the organisation.
4. Continuous Learning: Understand new cyberattacks and defensive techniques.
5. Real-World Experience: Learn how cyberattacks happen and how to defend against them.

> Protect the organisation by detecting, investigating, and responding to cyber threats efficiently.
