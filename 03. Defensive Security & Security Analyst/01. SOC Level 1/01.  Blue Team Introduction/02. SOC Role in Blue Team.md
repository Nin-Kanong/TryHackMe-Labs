#### Main Roles

| Role             | Responsibility                                        |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| CEO              | Oversees overall business objectives.                 |
| CISO             | Develops and manages the company's security strategy. |
| Security Manager | Manages security teams and daily operations.          |
| SOC Analyst      | Monitors, detects, and investigates cyber threats.    |

> The CEO focuses on business goals, while the CISO manages cybersecurity strategy and ensures security teams protect the organisation.


---


### Security Departments

Companies organise cybersecurity teams differently depending on their size.

- **Small Companies**: IT department handles cybersecurity.
- **Medium Companies**: A general Information Security team manages security tasks.
- **Large Companies**: A CISO oversees specialised security teams.

#### Main Security Teams

| Team      | Responsibility                                                                                  |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Red Team  | Performs penetration testing and simulates cyberattacks to identify vulnerabilities.            |
| GRC Team  | Manages Governance, Risk, and Compliance, including security policies and [PCI DSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payment_Card_Industry_Data_Security_Standard) requirements. |
| Blue Team | Defends company systems by monitoring, detecting, and responding to cyber threats.              |

### Simple Structure
````mermaid
flowchart TD

    R --> R1["Penetration Testing"]

````

> Red Team attacks to find weaknesses, Blue Team defends against attacks, and GRC manages security risks and compliance.

## Task 3: Meet the Blue Team

The **Blue Team** is responsible for defensive cybersecurity. Its main job is to continuously monitor systems, detect cyberattacks, investigate threats, and respond quickly to security incidents.

#### Key Responsibilities

1. **Monitoring**: Monitor networks, systems, and security alerts.
2. **Threat Detection**: Identify suspicious activities and attacks.
3. **Incident Response**: Investigate and contain security incidents.
4. **System Protection**: Protect company infrastructure and data.
5. **Continuous Improvement**: Improve security monitoring and defenses.

#### Team Structure

Depending on company size and industry, a Blue Team may have around 3–50 members across different roles and departments.

> The Blue Team protects the organisation by detecting, investigating, preventing, and responding to cyber threats.

### Security Operations Center (SOC)


<img width="5890" height="977" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b21e293a-14a3-40b1-9327-32c67117ccef" />


**SOC (Security Operations Center)** is the first line of defense against cyberattacks.

| Role         | Main Responsibility                          |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| L1 Analyst   | Monitors alerts and escalates complex cases. |
| L2 Analyst   | Investigates advanced attacks.               |
| SOC Engineer | Configures SIEM, EDR, and security tools.    |
| SOC Manager  | Manages the SOC team.                        |

> SOC detects, investigates, and responds to cyber threats.


---



### Cyber Incident Response Team (CIRT)


<img width="5890" height="977" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/edcab352-f447-4662-8076-d11c1302ad74" />

**CIRT** is a special team that handles serious cyberattacks when the SOC needs extra help.

- **[JPCERT](https://www.jpcert.or.jp/english)**: Japan's CERT handling nation-wide breaches
- **[Mandiant](https://www.mandiant.com/)**: A private team responding to global cyber incidents
- **[AWS CIRT](https://aws.amazon.com/security-incident-response)**: Investigates security incidents of AWS customers

## Task 4: Advancing SOC Career


### SOC Path

How to become a SOC Analyst (L1):

1. **Learn SOC Skills**: Study networking, SIEM, incident response, and threat detection.
2. **Practice**: Complete CTF challenges and follow cybersecurity news.
3. **Get Certified**: Consider the TryHackMe SAL1 certification.
4. **Apply for Jobs**: Prepare for interviews and apply for Junior SOC roles.
5. **Career Growth**: Gain experience and progress to L2, L3, or specialised security roles.

> ``Learn`` → ``Practice`` → ``Get Certified`` → ``Apply`` → ``Gain Experience`` → ``Advance``.


---



### Internal SOC vs MSSP

| Topic | Internal SOC | MSSP |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scenario Example** | You work in a SOC team of the bank and protect the bank's systems | You work for a global MSSP protecting its sixty customers in Europe |
| **Working Pace** | You usually have calm shifts without too much time pressure | Your shift usually starts from a queue of urgent alerts to analyze |
| **Security Tools** | You work with just a few tools, but need to know them very well | You have to work with sixty diverse security tools and platforms |
| **Incident Practice** | You saw and learned from just two major cyber attacks last year | Every week, you deal with attacks and breaches, and can learn from it |


**Note**:
- **Internal SOC**: Protects its own organisation.
- **MSSP**: Provides security services to multiple clients.

> Internal SOC focuses on one company, while MSSP protects many customers.


---

### Next Steps

After working as an L1 Analyst, we can choose different career paths:

- **SOC L2**: Investigate advanced security incidents.
- **SOC Engineer**: Build and manage security tools.
- **CIRT**: Handle major cyberattacks.
- **SOC Manager**: Manage security teams and operations.
- **CISO**: Lead the company's overall cybersecurity strategy.

> Spend our first 1–2 years gaining real-world experience, improving your skills, and exploring your preferred career path.
